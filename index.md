## About Me

I am a Ph.D. student at University of Leeds's [Visualization and Computer Graphics (VCG) group](https://vcg.leeds.ac.uk/), supervised by Dr. Rafael Kuffner dos Anjos, and Prof. Gordon Love. 

My current research interest lies at the intersection of human visual perception, computer graphics, and XR. Broadly, I enjoy exploring interactive systems that are user-centered, visually compelling and grounded in empirical studies of perception and behavior.

Previously, I received my MEng in Computer Science from Cornell Tech and BSc in Computer Science from University of Nottingham. I've conducted research under [Cornell XR Collaboratory](https://xrcollaboratory.tech.cornell.edu/) and [Texas A&M VVAI Lab](https://vvai-lab.github.io/), focusing on XR interaction and system design.
